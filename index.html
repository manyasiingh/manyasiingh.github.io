Biography

I am a Master’s student at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT) conducting research in speech signal processing, acoustic representations, and natural language processing. My work focuses on computational speech science—specifically automated acoustic segmentation, fundamental frequency ($F_0$) trajectory extraction, and Vowel Space Area (VSA) geometric metrics across multilingual datasets.

Alongside fundamental speech acoustics, I explore applied Generative AI systems, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, vector embeddings, and modular full-stack deployments using PyTorch, React, and Streamlit.
