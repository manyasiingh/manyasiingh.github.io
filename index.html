Research Projects

SSL Articulatory Feature Probing for Hindi Dysarthria

Probed frozen SSL representations (WavLM, XLS-R, IndicWav2Vec) for Hindi articulatory features using linear probing and leave-one-speaker-out validation. Found that Hindi-specific contrasts — retroflex/dental, four-way aspiration — degrade measurably under dysarthria; Hindi-aware pretraining (IndicWav2Vec) raises the ceiling but doesn't confer immunity.
