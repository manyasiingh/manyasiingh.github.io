Biography

I'm a Master's researcher at DA-IICT, working at the intersection of speech signal processing, acoustic phonetics, and natural language understanding. My current work focuses on automated formant extraction, fundamental frequency (F₀) dynamics, and geometric Vowel Space Area (VSA) metrics across multilingual corpora.

Beyond speech, I build generative AI systems — from Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines and vector databases to full-stack deployments with PyTorch, React, and Streamlit. I'm passionate about making acoustic analysis reproducible and interpretable.
