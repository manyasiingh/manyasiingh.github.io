01. About & Domain Focus

I am a Master's student specializing in speech processing and artificial intelligence. My primary research explores acoustic signal modeling—specifically fundamental frequency ($F_0$) trajectory tracking, automated acoustic segmentation, and Vowel Space Area (VSA) representation across multilingual speech datasets.

Beyond traditional speech signal analysis, I design and deploy deep learning architectures across NLP, Computer Vision, and Generative AI. I focus on bridging classical acoustic features with modern Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) frameworks, and production-ready full-stack interfaces.

Speech Processing

Formant tracking, pitch analysis, acoustic segmentation, VSA plotting.

NLP & LLM Systems
