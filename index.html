About

I'm an MSc IT graduate at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), working as a research intern at the Speech Research Lab, where I study how dysarthric speech can be represented in ways a clinician can actually interpret, from articulatory information in self-supervised models to biomarkers read directly off phoneme-posterior alignment paths.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of clinical speech-language pathology and representation learning: building features that a speech-language pathologist could actually use, not just a benchmark number.

Alongside this, I probe what general-purpose pretrained models do and don't encode, from steganographic signal in frozen vision encoders to reproducing recent activation-steering work for multilingual LLM reasoning, and tracking down the bugs that reproducibility studies tend to surface. Before DAU, I completed my BSc in Data Science at Navrachna University.

Encoding Articulatory Features in SSL Models for Hindi Dysarthric Speech
Manya Singh, Avantika Agarwal, Hemant A. Patil
IEEE ISCSLP 2026 · Penang, Malaysia
Accepted + WiML @ NeurIPS 2026

Probed WavLM, XLS-R and IndicWav2Vec for articulatory feature encoding in Hindi dysarthric speech; voicing survives dysarthria best, while Hindi-specific contrasts (retroflex/dental, four-way aspiration) degrade measurably under pathology. Extended work accepted at the Women in Machine Learning (WiML) Workshop at NeurIPS 2026, Sydney. Supported by MeitY, Government of India, under BHASHINI.

2026
Prosodic Features for Classification of Normal vs. Dysarthric Speech
Manya Singh, Avantika Agarwal, Hemant A. Patil
SPECOM 2026 · Ohrid, North Macedonia
Accepted

Phoneme-level prosodic analysis (pitch, energy, duration, VSA) with a Random Forest / XGBoost pipeline, reaching 84% accuracy and 0.92 AUC. Supported by MeitY, Government of India, under BHASHINI (National Language Translation Mission).

2026
Hidden in the Pixels, Lost in the Embedding? Cross-Architecture Probing of Steganographic Detectability
Manya Singh, Avantika Agarwal, Hemant A. Patil
ICVGIP 2026 · Kolkata, India
Accepted

Linear-probed five frozen vision encoders (CLIP ViT-B/32 and ViT-L/14, SigLIP, DINOv2, ResNet-50) for implicit sensitivity to LSB replacement and LSB matching. Detectability varies with training objective (AUC 0.99–1.00 for language-aligned encoders vs. 0.62 for DINOv2 at 4-bit full payload); all encoders stay near chance at realistic 1-bit payloads.
