Why read the path?

Forced aligners collapse an utterance into phone durations. The DTW path keeps how the speaker moved through each phone: where they lingered, skipped, or doubled back.

Drag the slider: as severity rises the path flattens into long horizontal dwells, the kind of pattern a clinician can point at.

Hidden in the Pixels, Lost in the Embedding? Cross-Architecture Probing of Steganographic Detectability
Manya Singh, Avantika Agarwal, Hemant A. Patil
ICVGIP 2026 · Kolkata, India
Accepted OpenReview ↗
  • Linear-probed five frozen vision encoders (CLIP ViT-B/32 and ViT-L/14, SigLIP, DINOv2, ResNet-50) for implicit sensitivity to LSB steganography, with noise controls and classical baselines.
  • Detectability varies with training objective (AUC 0.99–1.00 for language-aligned encoders vs. 0.62 for DINOv2 at 4-bit full payload); all encoders stay near chance at realistic 1-bit payloads.
